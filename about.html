Story time

We built SalesBot for our own agency, and realized… you should have it too

  1. 2017

    Blackhole Media starts

    Back in 2017, we launched Blackhole Media with a simple mission: kill off generic, fluff-filled B2B marketing and replace it with predictable lead flow.

  2. 2018

    The first SalesBot, built for ourselves

    To do that without losing our minds, we coded the initial version of SalesBot in 2018 to handle our internal LinkedIn outreach. It worked better than we ever imagined.

  3. 2018 – today

    Millions of personalized touches

    Over the next years, SalesBot was the engine behind millions of personalized touches and hundreds of millions in client pipeline.

  4. Now

    Rebuilt from the ground up for the AI era

    When old-school lead gen started slowing down and AI matured, we rebuilt SalesBot from the ground up. We replaced cold broadcast templates with deep company context, natural conversations, and strict compliance. Now, you don’t need a 16-person agency team to fill your calendar, you just need to switch SalesBot on.

Principles

A few non-negotiables

01 · Substance over spam

One thoughtful, relevant message will always beat a thousand cold templates.

02 · Outsource the grit, keep the relationship

Let the software handle the repetitive work; you jump in when it’s time to consolidate trust and sign contracts.

03 · Control and transparency

You choose how much autonomy SalesBot gets, with total visibility into every conversation and action.

04 · EU-first compliance

Automatic opt-out processing, strict local quiet hours (no 2 AM emails), safe daily sending limits, and GDPR-conscious data handling.
